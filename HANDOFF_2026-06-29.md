## Code Change That May Explain the Drop

`features/second_order.py` was **not in the codebase** at the Q3 baseline (committed Mar 28). It was added Apr 20, 2026 in commit `91a6d55`. It adds 15 features including several with high NaN rates:

- `arrival_delay_dispersion_near`: ~68% NaN
- `crossing_early_fraction`: ~45% NaN
- `core_to_periphery_tte_delta`: ~14.5% NaN

These sparse features are included in the kinematic and vessel_all arms of all new-code runs.
